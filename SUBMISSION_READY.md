# Handshake × Google AI Skills Studio Challenge: Submission Kit

**Contest Mission:** *Your Deadline, Your Design: Create a Countdown Lock Screen*  
**Prize:** $1,000 USD (Organized & Administered by Handshake)  
**Submission Deadline:** November 23, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. PT (November 24, about 1:30 p.m. IST)  

---

## 🎯 Submission Pitch Template
> "My deadline: [GOAL] on [DATE]. I planned backward with T-minus milestones: [MILESTONES]. I designed a [DEVICE] lock screen with clean space for the clock. [DESCRIBE WHAT YOU ACTUALLY DID IN GEMINI]."

*Example filled with app defaults:*
> "My deadline: AI-901 Exam on Nov 23, 2026. I planned backward with T-minus milestones: T-14 Start study guide; T-7 Foundry hands-on practice; T-3 Take practice test; T-1 Revise and check ID; T-0 AI-901 exam day. I designed a phone lock screen with clean space for the clock. [DESCRIBE WHAT YOU ACTUALLY DID IN GEMINI]."

## 📦 Project Deliverables

| Deliverable | Location | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Exported Wallpapers (Optional Examples)** | `assets/` | Optional exported lock screen images the user adds after exporting from the app. The app shows an informative placeholder card if they are missing. |
| **Interactive Studio Web App** | `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js` | Web application to customize goals, live preview mockups, export PNGs, and export `.ics` files. |
| **Calendar Reminders File** | `reminders.ics` | Timed 09:00 floating local time calendar events with same-day reminder alarms. |
| **Gemini Prompts & Chat** | `GEMINI_PROMPTS.md` | Prompts for planning, lock screen design, and daily reminders with placeholder for real chat. |

## 📝 Pre-Submission Checklist

Before submitting on Handshake, ensure you have completed each item:

- [ ] **Real Gemini chat done:** Ran the planning prompt in Google Gemini, answered the diagnostic questions, and pasted the transcript into `GEMINI_PROMPTS.md`.
- [ ] **Date shown on the lock screen matches my real, booked exam date**
- [ ] **Screenshot of the lock screen on my device shows no notifications or personal information**
- [ ] **Assets exist:** Lock screen image file is present in `assets/` or exported from the studio app.
- [ ] **Live link works on phone:** Web page or image opens properly on your mobile device.
- [ ] **No personal data:** Ensure no phone numbers, emails, or personal identification details appear on any generated image.
- [ ] **Submitted before the deadline:** Submit via the Handshake contest form before **November 23 at 11:59 p.m. PT** (November 24, about 1:30 p.m. IST).

---

## 🚀 How to Submit

1. **Set as Your Lock Screen:** Download your exported lock screen image and set it as your phone or laptop wallpaper. Take a photo or screenshot showing it active on your device.
2. **Submit to Handshake:** Visit the Handshake challenge submission page, upload your lock screen image/screenshot, and paste your filled-in pitch template into the description field.
3. **Add to Portfolio:** Link the project on your Handshake and professional profiles to demonstrate backward-planning and prompt design skills.
