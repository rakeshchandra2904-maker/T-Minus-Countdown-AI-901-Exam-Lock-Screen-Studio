## Part III: Accountability & Reminder Automation Prompts

### Daily Gemini Push Reminder Prompt:
```text
Every day at 9:00 AM until November 23, check the AI-901 Exam countdown in this chat, and send a push notification with any milestone scheduled for today or tomorrow. If no milestone is scheduled today, provide a 1-sentence productivity tip to keep momentum going.
```

### Google Calendar Integration Prompt:
```text
Add each milestone from our countdown above to my Google Calendar on its scheduled date, with an email and notification reminder set for 9:00 AM local time.
```

### Standup Accountability Coach Prompt:
```text
Act as my executive accountability partner. Each morning, ask me:
1. Did you check off yesterday's milestone for AI-901 Exam?
2. What is the single highest-priority task on your countdown today?
```
